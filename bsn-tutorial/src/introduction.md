# 1. Understanding Bevy Scene Notation (BSN)

Bevy Scene Notation is the latest feature of Bevy 0.19. It allows you to define and load scenes with a focus on composition and reusability via Templates.

This guide will walk you through the basics of BSN and how to use it in your Bevy projects as well as my approach to implementing reusable templates that can be shared throughout your game creation process.

I will attempt to be as comprehensive as possible in my explainations of the feature-set, however there may still be questions you have regarding concepts and/or intergration of edge cases. Or you may have  found something I did not account for in you production environment. If this is the case, please open an issue and submit feedback! I will do my best to read it as often as I can.
